About This Chart

This chart shows how the recession probability would evolve from now until December 31, 2025, assuming the base probability remains constant at 48.8%.

  • Blue filled area: Projected adjusted probability over time
  • Dashed teal line: Base probability (no temporal adjustment)
  • Red dot: Current position (today)

The adaptive method applies decay to probabilities below 40% and amplification to probabilities above 60%, with a smooth transition in between.
